Three Living Perspectives

  • 🌉 Bridge (Bible ↔ BOM): Witness how ancient Old Testament prophecies (Isaiah, Genesis) and New Testament events (Matthew, Luke) beam directly into Book of Mormon fulfillment.

Search any verse, filter internal or biblical arcs, and zoom into books

Instant Scripture Search & Filters

  • 🔍 Search Input: Type any reference (e.g., Alma 32:21, 1 Nephi 3:7) or thematic keyword to jump immediately.
  • ⚡ Scope Filters: Switch between All, Internal (BOM only), and Biblical (OT/NT parallels) to isolate connections.
  • 📖 Zoom into Specific Books: Select any of the 15 books in the dropdown to zoom into chapters and inspect localized cluster connections.

Alma 36: Symmetrical inverted parallelism centered on Christ (v. 18)

Ancient Semitic Craftsmanship

  • 📜 Hebrew Chiasmus: Explore ancient inverted poetry. The entire 30 verses of Alma 36 revolve symmetrically around the divine apex in verse 18: crying unto Jesus Christ for mercy.
  • ⭐ Interactive Apex & Levels: Click any level in the chiastic tree (A ↔ A', B ↔ B') to jump back and forth and compare paired parallel clauses.
  • ✍️ Prophetic Formulas: Discover Mormon's editorial signature ("And thus we see") and the Deuteronomy-rooted Covenant of the Promised Land ("Prosper in the land").
  • 🏛️ Hebraisms: Analyze authentic Semitic grammar: noun construct states ("plates of brass", "rod of iron"), solemn oaths ("As the Lord liveth"), and autobiographical colophons.
